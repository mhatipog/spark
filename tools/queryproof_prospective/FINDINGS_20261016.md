## Observations, with failures retained

- Each version: 3/5 historical issue reproducers returned results contradicting their independent SQL oracle. 2/5 did not reproduce. All 4/4 control SQLs were correct.
- Spark 4.2.0: 426 structured optimizer AST events parsed without error; the original structural heuristic flagged **0/3** reproduced bugs.
- Blinded per-rule optimizer exclusion: **1/3** confirmed wrong-results queries produced an alternative result (the excluded rule is `OptimizeJoinCondition`). It flagged **0/4** negative controls.
- Independently written SQL/control-configuration baseline: **3/3** confirmed wrong-results cases have a differing result, but this method *uses a manually designed reference* and therefore is not autonomous verification.
- One additional discrepancy occurred for the AQE sort case which returned the correct answer in baseline configuration. Excluding `InlineCTE` altered the plan and revealed a wrong final order.

## The previously masked AQE/global-ordering issue

A second experiment used **unmodified** official PySpark 4.0.1 and 4.2.0. There were eight repetitions per version for each of eight combinations of AQE, `InlineCTE` exclusion and broadcast thresholds: **128 executions**.

- 32 executions returned rows in the wrong order; 96 yielded the expected order.
- 64 final physical plan *text representations* lacked an explicit global `Sort` matching the logical root sort keys. The initial physical plan retained that sort.
- 32 of those 64 missing-sort cases still returned the expected order. This is not evidence that the ordering guarantee was satisfied.
- In the 32 wrong-order executions AQE was enabled, `InlineCTE` was excluded, and the final plan used `BroadcastHashJoin`. Removing the static **and** adaptive broadcast options selected `SortMergeJoin` and restored this specific two-row output sequence; the matching global `Sort` remained absent even then.
- This is an independently characterized variant of **already known SPARK-59126**, **not** a new discovered bug and **not** a proof which AQE rule is unsound.

## Go / no-go

**No-go on claiming a new general-purpose verifier.** The frozen structural verifier misses all three newly reproduced bugs. The ablation approach has limited coverage of mandatory subquery rewrites and physical AQE rules.

**Go on one narrowed research hypothesis:** a sound and explicit *translation-validation boundary* between the logical `Sort` requirement and the final adaptive physical plan's `outputOrdering` properties, with results TRUE/FALSE/UNKNOWN and adversarial negative controls. A text search for `Sort` is only a triage mechanism; it is not a semantic proof.

Neither SQLSolver, sqleq nor SQLancer was actually executed as an external end-to-end comparator in this study. Do not present the experiment as a superiority claim over those systems.
